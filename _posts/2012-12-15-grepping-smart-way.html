--- layout: post title: "grepping the smart way" date: 2012-12-15T10:14:00.001+01:00 permalink: /2012/12/grepping-smart-way.html blogger_id: "4114657045529949697" tags: ["grep"] --- Unfortunately WebLogic prints each log entry in multiple lines. This is a pain when you grep.

gawk will return the whole log entry:

gawk "BEGIN{RS=\"####\"}/BEA-000337/{print \$0}" *.log


When using grep, the option -A4 is handy to print a few extra lines (in the example, 4) after the ling matching the pattern:

grep -A4 BEA-000337 *
