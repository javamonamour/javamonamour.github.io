The broadcast address is always (?) the highest IP in the subnet range: 
Network: 172.25.1.64/26
Broadcast: 172.25.1.127
HostMin: 172.25.1.65
HostMax: 172.25.1.126
Hosts/Net: 62

and the gateway will be (broadcast-1) = 172.25.1.126
