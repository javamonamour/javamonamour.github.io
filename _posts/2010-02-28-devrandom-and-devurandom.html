--- layout: post title: "replace /dev/random with /dev/urandom" date: 2010-02-28T16:23:00.000+01:00 permalink: /2010/02/devrandom-and-devurandom.html blogger_id: "8823372925481697750" tags: ["random", "urandom"] --- /dev/random can really slow down WebLogic startup on Linux;
/dev/urandom is much faster

see here for a  solution
http://www.itonguard.com/20090313/weblogic-starts-slow/


add  “-Djava.security.egd=file:/dev/./urandom” (/dev/urandom does not work) to java parameters.
