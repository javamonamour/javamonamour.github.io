--- layout: post title: "DataSet annotation in Unitils" date: 2011-02-17T13:50:00.000+01:00 permalink: /2011/02/dataset-annotation-in-unitils.html blogger_id: "2609998734100534159" tags: ["DataSet", "unitils"] --- a cool feature is to initialize the DB before each test.

Since most data are common to all tests, it pays to factor them out to a shared DataSet (xml file)

Unitils supports this feature and this is the syntax:

@DataSet(value={"one.xml","two.xml"})
