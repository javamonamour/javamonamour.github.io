--- layout: post title: "Windows could not start the OracleXETNSListener service" date: 2010-12-01T12:15:00.000+01:00 permalink: /2010/12/windows-could-not-start.html blogger_id: "6936448939693537773" tags: ["ORACLE_HOME"] --- I had the smart idea of declaring ORACLE_HOME in the System Variables, as c:\Oracle1\Middleware . Apparently this interferes with the Oracle TNS Listener installed as a System Service.
Removing the variable ORACLE_HOME from the System area fixes the problem.... funny!
