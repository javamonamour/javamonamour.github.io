--- layout: post title: "Monitoring the content of a directory" date: 2009-02-17T18:41:00.001+01:00 permalink: /2009/02/monitoring-content-of-directory.html blogger_id: "4975142759606733895" --- if you want to keep under control the content of a directory, here is a quick and dirty Unix script:

while :; do echo `date`; ls -ltr .ssh; sleep 1; done

you can achieve the same with watch date; ls -ltr .ssh
