--- layout: post title: "OSB: difference between Route, Service Callout and Publish" date: 2010-04-28T10:52:00.000+02:00 permalink: /2010/04/osb-difference-between-route-service.html blogger_id: "5746559654897124662" tags: ["OSB"] --- service call out .. you call a service in a SYNCHRONOUS way (pipeline blocks and waits for service response)

publish ... same but ASYNCHRONOUS, so message flow continues in the pipeline

route ... it's the last node of the proxy, it's not in the pipeline, you pass all processing to another (business or proxy) service
