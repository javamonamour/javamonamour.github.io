--- layout: post title: "PL-SQL logging info with DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE in SQL Developer" date: 2012-08-15T17:29:00.002+02:00 permalink: /2012/08/pl-sql-logging-info-with.html blogger_id: "6766601175696867093" tags: ["PL-SQL"] --- unless you explicitly enable the logging, you will never see anything...

SET SERVEROUTPUT ON;

BEGIN

  DBMS_OUTPUT.ENABLE;
 DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('Hello...');

 DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('Goodbye.');
 END;
 /


in a stored procedure, just do

dbms_output.enable (1000000);
