As a rule, when things act weird:

- clear cookies
- rm -rf $DOMAIN_HOME/servers/$SERVER_NAME/tmp/
- rm -rf $DOMAIN_HOME/servers/$SERVER_NAME/cache/
- update webapp
- restart server



In Tomcat I never had all these problems.... mystery....
