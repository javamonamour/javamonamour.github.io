bcdtnf01.server.ux.acme.local:/data/nfs/tst/acme_tst_wls on /data/share/acme_tst_wls type nfs (rw,nosuid,hard,intr,addr=11.81.21.162)


then you might be exposed to the Linux NFS Mount Problem
