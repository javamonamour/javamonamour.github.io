--- layout: post title: "Cool way to synchronize you local folder with a remote folder" date: 2012-08-06T20:43:00.000+02:00 permalink: /2012/08/cool-way-to-synchronize-you-local.html blogger_id: "8864581273423949431" tags: ["WinSCP"] --- I hate developing code directly on the target machine - I would much rather edit them locally in Eclipse, scp them and checking them into SVN when they are stable.
So this utility provided by WinSCP is quite handy at copying remotely the files for me whenever I save them to the local folder:

WinSCP.exe soa@remotehost.com /keepuptodate C:\pierre\
workspace\myscripts /home/soa/targetdir/scripts

keepuptodate is the magic option
