--- layout: post title: "XQuery: how to chain (concat) 2 elements" date: 2011-12-19T15:40:00.004+01:00 permalink: /2011/12/xquery-how-to-chain-concat-2-elements.html blogger_id: "5866680569167064994" tags: ["XQuery"] --- curly braces will do the job:

let $a := <a>bla</a>
let $b := <b>blu</b>

return { $a, $b }


will return
<a>bla</a>
<b>blu</b>
