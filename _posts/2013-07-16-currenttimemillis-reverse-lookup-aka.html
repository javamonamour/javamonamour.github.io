--- layout: post title: "currentTimeMillis reverse lookup, AKA \"from currentTimeMillis to date\" in WLST" date: 2013-07-16T10:57:00.003+02:00 permalink: /2013/07/currenttimemillis-reverse-lookup-aka.html blogger_id: "544068212591270836" tags: ["date"] --- If you have a timestamp expressed as

System.currentTimeMillis()

=1373941644513  

you can transform it into a readable date this way:

from java.text import SimpleDateFormat 
from java.util import Date


sdf = SimpleDateFormat("MMM dd,yyyy HH:mm")
mydate = Date(1373941644513)
print sdf.format(mydate)
