--- layout: post title: "SchemaName in oracle.tip.adapter.db.DBStoredProcedureInteractionSpec (JCA DbAdapter)" date: 2013-04-06T22:11:00.002+02:00 permalink: /2013/04/schemaname-in-oracletipadapterdbdbstore.html blogger_id: "2390986384653052065" tags: ["DBAdapter"] --- In JDeveloper, the wizard automatically generates an element "property name="SchemaName" value="DEV1_SOAINFRA"" in the endpoint-interaction/interaction-spec className="oracle.tip.adapter.db.DBStoredProcedureInteractionSpec" element.
This is annoying since the schema is already associated to the JCA instance we are using (connection-factory location="eis/DB/Nesoa" UIConnectionName="DEV1_SOAINFRA" adapterRef="")
You can safely remove the SchemaName element.
It is also safe to rename UIConnectionName to anything you like.
