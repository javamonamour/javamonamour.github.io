--- layout: post title: "Oracle Unbreakable Linux vs Oracle Enterprise Linux vs Oracle Linux" date: 2015-03-04T07:27:00.001+01:00 permalink: /2015/03/oracle-unbreakable-linux-vs-oracle.html blogger_id: "3276216637681770696" tags: ["linux", "Oracle Linux"] --- I was really confused by all these product names... how are they different, and what is what?
It turns out that the reality is much simpler: the "Enterprise" qualifier is no longer used - just say "Oracle Linux" - although in the documentation the term "Oracle Enterprise Linux" is still widely used.
Also, the "Unbreakable" qualifier (honestly, a bit bumptious and uppish) is used only to refer to the KERNEL, and not to the OS itself...
