--- layout: post title: "Eclipse: open .sh files with text editor" date: 2013-11-28T16:46:00.001+01:00 permalink: /2013/11/eclipse-open-sh-files-with-text-editor.html blogger_id: "6660075205067214482" tags: ["eclipse"] --- Something annoying is that in Eclipse, when you open a .sh file, it will run an external editor to edit it.

Open Preferences, General/Editors/File Associations and add the extension (file type) .sh and associate the editor "Text Editor".
