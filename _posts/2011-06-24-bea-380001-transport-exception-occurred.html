--- layout: post title: "BEA-380001: A Transport Exception occurred during an SB Transport message processing" date: 2011-06-24T19:41:00.001+02:00 permalink: /2011/06/bea-380001-transport-exception-occurred.html blogger_id: "7486295055066862166" --- I was calling a BS from a PS1 (route) and I was always getting this error. The BS was using SB protocol to talk to another PS2.

It turned out that in PS1 I was inserting a wsa:SoapAction header.
Removing the header fixed the issue.
I am not sure at which point it was causing trouble.... one needs to turn on debug flags to trace operations.
