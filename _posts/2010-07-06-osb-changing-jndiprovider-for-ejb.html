Your solution worked for me." --- This is what happens if you change target to a EJB, then upgrade the JNDIProvider to point to the new target.... it looks like OSB is caching the remote reference to the EJB, and doesn't clear the cache when you change the JNDIProvider... this doesn't happen so often, but still it's an operational issue and the only workaround I could figure out is restarting the Admin and the MS!
The other solution is to disable JNDI Cache on the JNDIProvider.
