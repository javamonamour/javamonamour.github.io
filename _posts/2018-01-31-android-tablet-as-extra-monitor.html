--- layout: post title: "android tablet as extra monitor" date: 2018-01-31T20:55:00.001+01:00 permalink: /2018/01/android-tablet-as-extra-monitor.html blogger_id: "2824811717359374334" tags: ["tablet"] --- I am using SPACEDESK https://www.spacedesk.net/ as an extra monitor for my Fire Tablet. It's free and it works seamlessly as it was an extra monitor. I have configured it as 3rd monitor, because I already had one. You can position it with Windows 10 "display settings" as any other monitor.

Once in a while it crashes, but not so often....

Priceless!
