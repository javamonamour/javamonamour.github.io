--- layout: post title: "Xlib: PuTTY X11 proxy: MIT-MAGIC-COOKIE-1 data did not match" date: 2014-01-27T15:56:00.000+01:00 permalink: /2014/01/xlib-putty-x11-proxy-mit-magic-cookie-1.html blogger_id: "244600828237322925" tags: ["X11"] --- I got this message logging as user1 with X11 forwarding with putty, then sudo su - user2, then run JConsole
solution:
login as user1
xauth list
(you get here a list of entries, say line1, line2...)
sudo su - user2
xauth add line1
xauth add line2
It works!
