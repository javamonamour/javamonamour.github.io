--- layout: post title: "puppet-lint: how to sort your diagnostics" date: 2013-10-07T12:37:00.001+02:00 permalink: /2013/10/puppet-lint-how-to-sort-your-diagnostics.html blogger_id: "8987888308706896349" tags: ["puppet"] --- I find a bit irritating that the output of puppet-lint is not sorted. I refer to fix my puppet manifests top-down rather than by class of error. This does the job: 
puppet-lint /vagrant/modules/mymodule/manifests/mymanifest.pp | awk '{ print $NF , $0}' | sort -n
and the result is neatly sorted by linenumber:
