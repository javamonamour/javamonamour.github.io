--- layout: post title: "Restarting your remote Application Server from Windows, with WinSCP" date: 2011-03-24T13:53:00.001+01:00 permalink: /2011/03/restarting-your-remote-application.html blogger_id: "7308190181227615086" tags: ["WinSCP"] --- "c:\Program Files\WinSCP\WinSCP.com" /script=restart.script

and restart.script is:

option confirm off
open username:password@hostname
cd /pathToYourStartStopScripts
call ./stop.sh
call ./start.sh




easy does it!

No more need to putty to your remote AppServer host to recycle the server
